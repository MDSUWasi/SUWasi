Privacy Policy

A clear explanation of the limited analytics used on this portfolio and what to expect when following external links.

Website analytics

This website uses Umami Analytics and Google Search Console to understand how the site is found and which pages are visited. This information helps improve the portfolio and its content.

Umami is configured for privacy-focused, aggregated website measurement. The site owner does not use these analytics for advertising or sell visitor analytics data. Google Search Console provides site-performance information to the site owner; it is not a visitor-facing feature.

Cookies and personal information

This site does not intentionally use analytics cookies to identify visitors, and it does not ask visitors to submit personal information through the homepage. The site's theme preference may be stored locally in your browser so the display setting can be remembered.

Analytics services may process technical request information as needed to provide their services. Please consult their current privacy documentation for details about their processing and retention practices.

External websites

Pages on this site may link to third-party websites and services. Once you follow an external link, that site's own privacy policy, terms, and analytics practices apply. This policy does not cover those external services.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the site or its analytics practices change. For questions about this policy, contact shafiun.wasi@outlook.com.
